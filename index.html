Career Navigator is built on a modern full-stack architecture — a Flutter mobile frontend communicating with a Python/Flask REST API, backed by a 28-table MySQL database with 9 triggers and 8 stored procedures. Real-time chat is powered by Socket.IO, media by Cloudinary, and intelligence by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash.
